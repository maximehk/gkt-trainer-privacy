# Privacy Policy – GKT Zürich Trainer

_Last updated: 2 October 2026_

This app is an independent study aid for the Grundkenntnistest (GKT) of the Canton of Zürich. It is not published by, affiliated with, or endorsed by the Canton of Zürich or any government body.

## Data we collect

None. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not operate a server that receives your data.

## Data stored on your device

Your answers, review schedule, saved questions and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data. If you use device backups (for example iCloud Backup), your backup may include it under Apple's terms.

## When data leaves the app

Only when you choose to:

- **Ask AI / Share:** sends the question text shown on screen, plus any context you type, to the app or service you pick (for example Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Messages). Their own privacy policies apply. Nothing is sent without your action.
- **Export progress:** creates a file with your progress that you can save or share.
- **Notifications:** an optional daily reminder, scheduled locally on your device. Notification permission is requested only if you turn it on.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
